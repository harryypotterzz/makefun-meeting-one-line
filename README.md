## The line

**This meeting could have been a one-line email, but the calendar needed a hostage.**

One sentence, 81 characters, no quotes. The decision was already email-sized; the only reason the meeting exists is the calendar invite. It is not a copy of the sponsor's target sentence "Let's just email the decision and move on." or the example "Could this meeting have been a one-line email instead?"
